Common questions

How do I play against a friend?

Tap VERSUS on the title screen. Apple's Game Center screen opens, with a quick match option and an invite option. Inviting sends them a link by message — they do not need to be a Game Center friend, and they do not need the game installed to receive it.

My friends list is empty

Apple only shows friends who are Game Center friends with you and have opened Word Wars themselves. On a new account it will usually be empty, and that is normal rather than broken. Use the invite option instead — it reaches anyone in your contacts.

I bought the Premium pack and it is gone

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. The pack is tied to your Apple Account, so it will come back on any device signed into the same one. If it does not, email us with the date of purchase.

A match connected and then nothing happened

Both players have to be on the same version. If one of you is on an older build, update and try again. If it persists, let us know roughly when it happened.

Can I turn off the swearing?

The profanity filter is on by default and masks rude words on screen. You can switch it off in Settings. Slurs are not in the dictionary at all and cannot be played.

How do I get rid of the adverts?

The Premium pack removes the break between matches, and adds three cosmetics you cannot unlock by playing.

Privacy

Word Wars has no servers and collects nothing about you. The full detail, including what Google's advertising software collects, is in the Privacy Policy.
